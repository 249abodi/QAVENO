Platform

Everything your business needs in one place

From the front counter to the back office, QAVENO keeps sales, stock and people connected.

Point of Sale

Fast checkout with barcode scanning, keyboard shortcuts and receipt printing — built to keep a queue moving.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, weighted-average costing, reorder alerts and a complete audit trail.

Multi-Branch

Centralized control across branches with inter-branch transfers, per-branch stock and role-based access.

Analytics & Reports

Sales trends, profit analysis and product performance with a real-time dashboard of the metrics that matter.

Purchasing

Purchase orders, goods receiving, supplier management and damaged-goods tracking with full history.

Stock Transfers

Inter-branch dispatch and receive with partial transfers and weighted-average cost blending.

AI Insights

Demand forecasting, anomaly detection, auto-reorder suggestions and slow-mover identification.

Offline-First

Keep selling through outages with a local database. Optional cloud sync reconnects when you do.

Role-Based Access

Owner, admin, manager and cashier roles with granular permissions, branch-level control and audit logging.
